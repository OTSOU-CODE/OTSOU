How We Work

The 3-Step Process

From initial sketch to the final precision stitch, discover how we transform your vehicle's cabin.

01

1. Consult & Design

We sit down with you to discuss your vision, inspect your vehicle, and select the ideal materials (Nappa leather, Alcantara, thread color) to match your taste.

02

2. Tailoring & Sourcing

We procure original automotive-grade skins and use digital templating to trace and cut the fabric. Every pattern is customized for a factory-perfect fit.

03

3. Master Assembly

Our master artisans hand-stitch and reconstruct your seats, dashboard, or roof in our workshop. We perform a multi-point quality check before hand-over.
