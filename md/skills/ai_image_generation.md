# Skill: AI Image Prompt Engineering (8K Automotive Realism)

## Overview

This skill governs the precise, technical methodology required to instruct AI models to generate hyper-realistic, 8K automotive studio photography. It relies heavily on strict adherence to the `docs.md` specifications to ensure consistency, physical accuracy, and photorealism.

## 1. The Realism Mandate

The ultimate goal is to generate imagery indistinguishable from reality. This requires moving beyond simple descriptions into explicit photographic technical directions.

- **Rejecting the "Artistic"**: Actively append negative prompts eliminating "concept art," "3d render," "unreal engine," "oversaturated," and "distorted."

## 2. Structuring the Prompt Architecture

Every prompt must be systematically built using these core pillars:

### A. Camera & Lens Specifications

- **Resolution Directives**: Explicitly request 8K, 50MP+, high-megapixel capture.
- **Focal Length Control**: Dictate specific lenses (`shot on 100mm lens`, `135mm prime`) to enforce realistic perspective compression and eliminate wide-angle bowing.
- **Aperture & Exposure**: Define the depth of field (`f/8 to f/11`) to ensure the entire car is sharply in focus, utilizing base ISO (`ISO 100`) for zero digital noise.

### B. Composition & Staging

- **The 'Hero' Cut**: Instruct the AI that the vehicle must be "100% visible in frame," "no cropping of bumpers or roof."
- **Angle Precision**: "45-degree front 3/4 angle," "camera height at 24 inches (headlight level)."

### C. Environmental Physics & Lighting

- **Studio Setup**: "Shot in professional infinity cove studio," "gloss white epoxy floor."
- **Lighting Architecture**: Request "massive overhead skylight softbox," "subtle front fill light."
- **Crucial Reflections & Shadows**: The prompt **must** demand "realistic contact shadows under tires" and "rectangular softbox reflection in windshield."

### D. Extreme Detail Enforcement (The 400% Zoom Test)

- **Logos & Badging**: "Ultra-sharp manufacturer logo on front grille," "perfectly legible wheel center caps."
- **Textures**: Mention "visible brake rotor texture," "metallic flake in dark blue paint."

## 3. Blueprint Adherence

- When generating specific models (e.g., Dacia Logan), the prompt must explicitly constrain the AI to real-world proportions, instructing it to strictly follow manufacturing visual guidelines, rejecting any generative hallucinatory "widebody" or inaccurate adaptations.
